Our Journey

A Decade of Building India's Wireless Future

From a prototype LoRa module in a small Gujarat lab to powering millions of smart devices — here's how Nebulae grew.

10+ Years
of continuous IoT hardware innovation from Anand, Gujarat
2013 Founded
Nebulae is Founded in Anand, Gujarat
A team of embedded systems engineers founds Nebulae with a single goal: build India's first locally-manufactured, WPC-approved wireless IoT module. The first LoRa module prototype is completed within 6 months.
2015
First LoRa Module — WPC Type Approval
Nebulae becomes one of India's first companies to receive WPC (Wireless Planning & Coordination) type approval for a LoRa module. First commercial deployments for smart water metering in Gujarat launch.
2017
Wi-SUN Module Launch & BIS Certification
Nebulae launches India's first indigenously-designed Wi-SUN FAN module, earning BIS certification. Partnership with state electricity boards for AMI pilot deployments begins across Gujarat and Maharashtra.
2019 Milestone
100,000 Devices Deployed — Zigbee & BLE Added
Nebulae crosses 100,000 deployed IoT endpoints. The product portfolio expands with Zigbee and BLE modules. ISO 9001:2015 quality certification is achieved. Team grows to 40+ engineers.
2021
Smart City Deployments & RDSS Programme Entry
Nebulae modules are selected for smart street lighting projects across 3 Indian cities. Company begins supplying Wi-SUN modules for RDSS (Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme) smart meter rollout under Ministry of Power.
2023
Tuya IoT Partnership & International Expansion
Nebulae becomes an authorised Tuya IoT Developer Partner. CE and FCC certifications for export-grade modules enable shipments to 40+ countries. Wi-Fi and GSM/LTE portfolios are launched. Team reaches 60+ engineers.
2025 Today
1M+ Devices. 500+ Products. Pan-India Reach.
Nebulae now offers 500+ IoT products across 6 protocols, serving 200+ enterprise clients. Over 1 million Nebulae-powered devices are active across India — in smart meters, city infrastructure, agriculture, and industrial plants.
Technology DNA

What Makes Nebulae Different

Six core engineering pillars that set Nebulae hardware apart from off-the-shelf wireless modules.

01
In-House RF Engineering

Every Nebulae module is designed by our in-house RF team — antenna design, impedance matching, and EMC pre-compliance testing done internally before production. No reference-design shortcuts.

02
Full Indian Regulatory Compliance

WPC type approval, BIS certification, and TEC compliance managed in-house. Our regulatory team ensures every product is legal to deploy in India — eliminating risk for our customers.

03
Complete Firmware & SDK Support

Every module ships with production-grade firmware, schematic source files, AT command documentation, and application examples — cutting customer development time from months to days.

04
Industrial Temperature Range

All modules are rated for –40°C to +85°C operating temperature — suitable for outdoor smart city deployments, industrial environments, and harsh field conditions without thermal derating.

05
Ultra-Low Power Architecture

Purpose-designed low-power sleep states deliver 10+ year battery life on LoRa modules — enabling coin-cell powered smart meters, soil sensors, and asset trackers without maintenance cycles.

06
Dedicated Engineering Support

Every customer gets direct access to the same engineers who designed the hardware — for architecture review, protocol selection, firmware debugging, and field deployment support.

Certifications & Compliance

Certified for Every Market

All Nebulae products carry relevant regulatory certifications — ensuring seamless deployment across India and internationally.

WPC
WPC License
Equipment Type Approval (ETA) from India's DoT Wireless Planning & Coordination Wing — required for all wireless products sold or imported in India, including LoRa, Wi-SUN, BLE & Wi-Fi devices
BIS
BIS Certification
Bureau of Indian Standards compliance for electronics sold in India — ensures product safety and quality against Indian national standards (verification pending)
CE
CE Marking
European conformity under the Radio Equipment Directive (RED) 2014/53/EU — Nebulae modules carry CE marking, with CE-RED compliance confirmed across the product range
FCC
FCC Certified
FCC Part 15 certified — Nebulae's LoRa concentrator boards and mini PCIe cards are FCC-certified for USA deployment and global export markets
ISO
ISO 9001:2015
ISO 9001:2015 quality management system — covering SLS's design, manufacturing, and support processes across the GIDC facility (verification pending)
LoRa
LoRa Alliance
Nebulae's LoRaWAN products are compliant with LoRa Alliance specifications — the LoRaWAN stack, FOTA, and network server are built to LoRa Alliance standard, with devices supporting Class A/B/C interoperability
WSA
Wi-SUN Alliance
Wi-SUN FAN (Field Area Network) compliant modules for smart metering & smart cities — Nebulae's Wi-SUN product range is built to Wi-SUN Alliance specifications, interoperable with all certified FAN devices
IS
IS 16444
Indian Standard IS 16444 for Advanced Metering Infrastructure — Nebulae's Wi-SUN modules target compliance with India's MoP RDSS scheme for smart meter rollout (verification pending)
