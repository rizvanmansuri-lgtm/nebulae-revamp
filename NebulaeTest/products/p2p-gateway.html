About P2P Gateway

The P2P Single Channel LoRa Gateway is a compact and cost-effective gateway designed for reliable long-range point-to-point communication using LoRa technology. Operating on a single frequency channel, it enables direct device connectivity without the complexity of a full LoRaWAN network.

It is based on a Cortex-M4 STM32WL 32-bit RISC processor at 48 MHz with 256 KB flash and 64 KB RAM. RF parameters such as frequency, spreading factor and bandwidth are configurable, receiver sensitivity is down to −137 dBm at SF12 / 125 kHz, and conducted power is +27 dBm.

Data is carried over a 4G LTE connection (Nano SIM tray, external 4G antenna), and the gateway is powered from a 5 V / 3 A DC supply. It ships in a polycarbonate wall-mount enclosure of 135 × 90 × 65 mm and uses a WPC certified LoRa module.

* Range and node count are estimated based on ideal conditions with limited sources of interference; field results will vary.
