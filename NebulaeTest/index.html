Six Protocols. One Ecosystem.

Nebulae's complete wireless connectivity portfolio spans every use-case — from kilometre-range low-power IoT to high-throughput industrial automation.

LoRa / LoRaWAN

Long Range · LPWAN

Ultra-long range (up to 15km), ultra-low power wireless communication ideal for smart metering, asset tracking, and large-scale IoT deployments.

View LoRa Products

Wi-SUN

Smart Utility Network

IEEE 802.15.4g-based mesh networking standard purpose-built for smart grids, electric meters, and large-scale smart city utility deployments.

View Wi-SUN Products

Zigbee

Mesh Networking · Home IoT

IEEE 802.15.4 mesh protocol offering reliable, self-healing networks for smart home, building automation, and industrial sensor networks.

View Zigbee Products

Wi-Fi

Wireless LAN · High Throughput

High-bandwidth 2.4GHz/5GHz modules for IoT gateways, edge devices, and applications requiring rich data throughput and cloud connectivity.

GSM / Cellular

2G · 4G LTE · NB-IoT

Nebulae IoT Solutions Across Every Sector

End-to-end wireless connectivity solutions built on LoRa, Wi-SUN, and Zigbee — deployed across utilities, cities, industries, and homes.

Advanced Metering Infrastructure (AMI)

Nebulae's AMI solution delivers two-way communication between smart meters and the head-end system using LoRaWAN and Wi-SUN FAN protocols. Fully compliant with India's RDSS scheme and IS 16444 standard, it enables 15-minute interval data, remote disconnect/reconnect, tamper alerts, and demand-response — for electricity, water, and gas utilities at scale.

Key Capabilities

Two-way AMR / AMI

Automatic meter reading with 15-min interval data, time-of-use tariff support and real-time load profiling over LoRa or Wi-SUN.

Tamper & Fraud Detection

Magnetic field, cover-open, reverse energy, and neutral disturbance detection with instant cloud alerts — reducing T&D losses by 25%.

Remote Disconnect / Reconnect

OTA-triggered load switch for instant disconnection on non-payment and reconnection upon settlement — no field visit needed.

MDMS & HES Integration

Pre-integrated with major Meter Data Management Systems via DLMS/COSEM, REST API, and MQTT for seamless DISCOM backend connectivity.

Nebulae Products Used
LoRa Module (NB-LRM series) — meter endpoint
Wi-SUN Module (NB-WSM series) — IS 16444 AMI
LoRa Concentrator — DCU / data collector unit
Wi-SUN Gateway — head-end server uplink
Smart City Infrastructure

Nebulae powers next-generation smart city deployments — from adaptive street lighting and parking sensors to environmental monitoring and solid waste management. Our LoRa and Wi-SUN networks connect thousands of city nodes on a single gateway, with centralised SCADA control, real-time dashboards, and open API integration for Smart City Mission and AMRUT 2.0 compliance.

Key Capabilities

Adaptive Street Lighting

Dimming schedules, fault detection, energy metering per pole, and centralised SCADA control over Wi-SUN mesh — achieving 40–50% energy savings.

Environmental Monitoring

Air quality (PM2.5, CO2, NO2), noise, and weather stations across the city providing real-time pollution maps and regulatory reports.

Transformer Monitoring

Current, voltage, temperature, and oil-level sensors on distribution transformers — preventing failures and enabling predictive maintenance for DISCOMs.

Solid Waste & Parking

Ultrasonic fill-level sensors in smart bins trigger optimised collection routes; LoRa parking sensors reduce congestion in city centres.

Nebulae IIoT connects factory machinery, conveyor systems, environmental sensors, and safety devices into a real-time OT/IT integrated network. Key Capabilities

Machine Condition Monitoring

Environmental & Safety Monitoring

Asset & Inventory Tracking

Building Management System (BMS)

Nebulae's BMS solution integrates HVAC, lighting, access control, fire safety, and energy management into one unified wireless network using Zigbee mesh and Wi-SUN. Facility managers get a single-pane web dashboard with predictive maintenance alerts, zone-based occupancy automation, and ISO 50001-ready energy reports — all without running new cabling.

Key Capabilities

HVAC & Lighting Automation

Smart Access Control

Fire & Safety Integration

Energy Analytics & ISO 50001
