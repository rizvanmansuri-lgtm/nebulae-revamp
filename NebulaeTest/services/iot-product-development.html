How We Work

Our Development Process

A structured 6-phase process with clear milestones, shared Jira board, and weekly review calls — so you always know where your product stands.

1

Discovery & Feasibility

Requirements gathering, BOM cost estimate, risk assessment — 1 week.

2

Schematic & Firmware Architecture

Complete schematic + firmware architecture doc for client sign-off.

3

PCB Layout & Prototype Order

Gerber files sent to fab; first prototype boards in 3–4 weeks.

4

Bring-Up & Functional Testing

Power sequencing, RF characterisation, protocol stack validation.

5

Regulatory Testing

Pre-compliance EMC sweep; certification submission management.

6

Production Handoff

CM-ready Gerber + BOM + test jig; pilot run supervision.
