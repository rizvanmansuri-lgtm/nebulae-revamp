Nebulae Pravah 15 Smart Water Meter

The LoRa-based smart water meter turns traditional metering into a connected, data-driven system for smart-city infrastructure. Low-power, long-range LoRa communication carries readings across wide urban and rural areas while the meter runs for years on a single battery.

It reports to a centralised Head End System, which feeds dashboards, billing and the consumer mobile app.

  • Rugged design — weather-resistant meter with optimised power management and encrypted data transmission
  • Open to HES — seamless integration with the Head End System for data management and billing

Smart Electricity Metering (AMI)

Nebulae supplies the hardware and software layers of an Advanced Metering Infrastructure: Wi-SUN protocol modules, NIC cards and DCUs in the field, and a Network Management Server, Head End System and mobile app on top.

The NM0221-I Wi-SUN module is WPC certified and suited to IS 16444 smart-metering applications; its self-forming, self-healing mesh and FOTA capability allow remote security updates.

Automatic Meter Reading for Gas Meters

Nebulae AMR is a wireless, battery-based device for live reporting of gas consumption, with a built-in pulse detector. It installs on existing diaphragm gas meters and sends data through a single LoRaWAN gateway / DCU to Nebulae or another provider's LoRaWAN server.

It is built on the NebuLink node NLN500a, described as the first and smallest STM32WL-based LoRaWAN module.

Hall effectPulse counting
IP65+Ingress protection
−20 to +85 °COperating range
  • Plug-and-play retrofit — easy hook-on with screw or cable-tie; no configuration needed, sends live data at pre-configured intervals
  • Accurate reading — incremental pulse count using Hall-effect sensing; the sensor stays on and wakes the device from sleep on a pulse
  • Tamper proof — device-removal and magnetic-interference alerts; other operations can be disabled on a tamper event (configurable)
  • Long battery life — replaceable non-rechargeable D-cell (Li-SOCl₂), 5+ years with deep-sleep technology
  • No walk-by or drive-by — client–server model with multipoint communication, up to 10 km line of sight per gateway

Smart Street Lighting System

The controller manages ON/OFF schedules, monitors temperature and offers over- and under-voltage protection. It measures power, energy, current and voltage so energy consumption can be tracked and optimised.

Three product families cover every installation: pole-mount / retrofit controllers, NEMA (7-pin) intelligent lighting controllers with LoRaWAN or 4G, and CCMS feeder panels — all managed from the ViaLights LoRa Management System.

Distribution Transformer Monitoring System

Conventional monitoring relies on periodic inspections and site visits with portable instruments — slow, dependent on skills and equipment, and a cause of downtime and transformer burn-outs. The DTMS measures electrical parameters and field sensors continuously and sends them to a central server.

It takes its supply from any phase of the DT secondary, so no additional power input is required.

GSMBuilt-in module
3-phase 4-wireCompatible
4–20 mAExtra field sensors
  • Electrical parameters — three-phase voltage, current, frequency, phase angle, active / reactive / apparent power, power factor, THD and harmonics up to 60
  • Physical parameters — oil temperature, winding temperature, oil level, LV-side lug temperature
  • Rogowski coil sensors — clamp-on installation without shutdown, up to 2500 A
  • Alarm & fault management — predicts abnormal conditions, raises alarms for over-voltage, over-current and phase imbalance
  • Connectivity — GSM, GPS, Bluetooth, RS-485 and Ethernet interfaces
