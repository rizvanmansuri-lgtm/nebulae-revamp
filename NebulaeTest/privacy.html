Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 June 2026

This policy explains how Nebulae IoT (Nebulae Technologies Pvt. Ltd.) collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you visit our website or interact with us — in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and GDPR.

In plain English: We collect only the data we need to respond to your enquiries, operate our website, and improve our products. We don't sell your data. We don't use it for advertising profiling. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

1. Data We Collect

Information you give us directly

  • Contact forms: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and the message you send via our website contact or quote forms.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: Email address and your name (optional).
  • Job applications: Name, email, phone, CV/resume, work history, and any other information you include in your application.
  • Event registrations: Name, email, job title, and company for events or webinars we organise.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: Pages visited, time on page, referring URL, browser type, operating system, and device type — collected via cookies and server logs.
  • IP address: Collected for security monitoring, geo-analytics (country/city level only), and fraud prevention.
  • Analytics: Aggregate, anonymised data about how visitors use our website — collected via Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymisation enabled.

Information we do NOT collect

  • Payment card numbers or banking details (we don't process payments on this website)
  • Government-issued ID numbers
  • Biometric or health data
  • Data from children under 18 (see Section 9)

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data only for the following purposes:

  • Responding to enquiries: To reply to contact form submissions, quote requests, and support queries.
  • Project delivery: To communicate with you during active projects — hardware orders, deployment, and integration work.
  • Marketing communications: To send newsletters, product updates, event invitations, and case studies — only if you've opted in or have an existing business relationship with us. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Website improvement: To understand how visitors use our website and improve content, navigation, and performance.
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable laws, court orders, or regulatory requirements.
  • Security: To monitor for and respond to security threats, fraud, and abuse of our systems.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or significant effects on you.

3. Cookies

We use cookies to operate the website and understand how it's used. The types of cookies we use:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: Required for the website to function — session management, security tokens. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics 4 with anonymised IP — help us understand page popularity and visitor flow. You can opt out via our cookie consent banner or the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your dark mode preference and cookie consent choice.

We do not use advertising, tracking, or third-party retargeting cookies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Service providers: Third-party tools we use to operate the website — email service provider (for newsletter delivery), web hosting provider, and analytics platform (Google Analytics). All are contractually bound to process data only on our instructions.
  • Legal authorities: When required by applicable law, court order, or regulatory authority (e.g. CERT-In directives under India's IT Act).
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets — subject to the same privacy commitments.

All third-party service providers are required to implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

5. Data Retention

  • Contact form enquiries: Retained for 3 years from last interaction, then deleted.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: Retained until you unsubscribe, then deleted within 30 days.
  • Job applications: Retained for 12 months after the position is filled, then securely deleted unless you consent to longer retention for future roles.
  • Project correspondence: Retained for 7 years in line with Indian commercial record-keeping requirements under the Companies Act 2013.
  • Analytics data: Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months (the minimum configurable period), then automatically deleted by Google.
  • Server logs: Retained for 90 days for security monitoring, then deleted.

6. Security

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction:

  • All data transmitted to and from our website is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or TLS 1.3 (HTTPS).
  • Access to systems containing personal data is restricted to authorised personnel only, using role-based access control.
  • Passwords and credentials are stored using industry-standard cryptographic hashing.
  • We conduct periodic security reviews and maintain a vulnerability disclosure process.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately at privacy@nebulae.io.

7. Your Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDPA) and, where applicable, the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: Ask us to delete your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time (without affecting prior lawful processing).
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Lodge a complaint with our Data Protection Officer or the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate a person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity (DPDPA).

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@nebulae.io. We will respond within 30 days.

To unsubscribe from marketing emails: Click the "Unsubscribe" link in any email we send, or email privacy@nebulae.io with the subject "Unsubscribe". We'll process your request within 5 business days.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us at privacy@nebulae.io and we will delete it promptly.

9. International Data Transfers

Nebulae IoT is headquartered in Anand, Gujarat, India. Your data is primarily stored and processed in India. Some of our third-party service providers (such as Google Analytics) may process data outside India. Where this occurs, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with DPDPA requirements and applicable data transfer mechanisms.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or for other operational reasons. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

For any privacy-related questions, requests, or complaints:

  • Email: privacy@nebulae.io
  • Post: Data Protection Officer, Nebulae IoT, Anand, Gujarat 388001, India
  • Response time: We aim to respond to all requests within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India once established under the DPDPA 2023, or with your local data protection authority if you are based in the EU/UK.
