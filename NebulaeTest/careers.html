We're not a typical startup. We're an engineering company that makes real hardware for real deployments — and we hire people who care about that distinction.

Engineer-Led Culture

Every decision is made by people who understand the hardware. No bureaucracy between your idea and shipping it.

Real Products, Real Scale

Your work goes into products deployed across 50,000+ IoT nodes in Indian cities, utilities, and farms — not internal tools.

Learning Budget

₹30,000/year for courses, certifications (WPC, BIS, AWS), conference attendance, and technical books. No approval committee.

Flexible Work

Our Hiring Process

Simple, Fast, Respectful

From application to offer in under 3 weeks. No ghosting — we respond to every application.

1

CV Review

We review your application within 5 business days. No automated screening — a human engineer reads every CV.

2

Technical Screen

30-min async technical task or code review — relevant to the actual role, not algorithmic puzzles.

3

Engineering Interview

60-min call with the team — technical depth, past projects, and how you think through real problems.

4

Founder Call

30-min call with our CTO — culture fit, your questions about Nebulae, and offer discussion.

5

Offer

Written offer within 48 hours of the final interview.
