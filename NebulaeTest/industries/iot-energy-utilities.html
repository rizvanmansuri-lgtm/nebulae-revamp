One Vendor from Meter to Head End

Nebulae supplies the radio modules, NIC cards, data concentrators and AMR devices that connect meters, and the Network Management and Head End software that collects the data.

Electricity meters follow IS 16444 over Wi-SUN or RF; gas and water meters use battery-powered LoRaWAN AMR devices. Distribution transformers are monitored with the DTMS.

Why it matters

Challenges We Help Solve

1

Meters spread over wide, mixed areas

How Nebulae helpsWi-SUN mesh for dense electricity-meter clusters and LoRaWAN for gas and water meters that are far apart, so each network fits its terrain.

2

Gas and water meters have no mains power

How Nebulae helpsBattery-powered AMR devices with built-in pulse detection and a battery life of 5+ years, so nobody needs to climb to the meter for years.

3

Data has to reach billing and analytics systems

How Nebulae helpsThe Head End Systems collect readings (DLMS/COSEM for IS 16444 meters) and pass them on through REST APIs to MDM and billing.

4

Transformers fail without warning

How Nebulae helpsThe DTMS measures electrical and physical parameters and raises alarms for over-voltage, over-current and phase imbalance.

FAQ

Common Questions

More answers are in our full FAQ, or ask our team.

Which meters does Nebulae support?

IS 16444 electricity meters over Wi-SUN or sub-GHz RF, and gas and water meters through LoRaWAN or Sigfox AMR devices. Water meters can be magnetic, inductive or RS485 types.

How many meters can one Wi-SUN data concentrator handle?

The NLG8520 supports up to 250 nodes and the NLG6770-IN up to 200 nodes. The exact number in your network depends on topology and reporting interval, so we plan it with you.

How long does the gas or water AMR battery last?

The AMR devices are designed for a battery life of 5+ years. Actual life depends on how often readings are sent.

Can the Head End connect to our billing system?

Yes. The HES exposes REST APIs and standard connectors for third-party MDM and billing systems.

Can you monitor existing transformers without a shutdown?

The DTMS can use clamp-on Rogowski coil sensors (up to 2500 A), which are fitted without switching the transformer off.
