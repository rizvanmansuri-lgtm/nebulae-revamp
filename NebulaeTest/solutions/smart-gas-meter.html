Smart Gas Metering & AMR/AMI

Nebulae's gas metering solution attaches a LoRaWAN-enabled pulse adapter to any OIML-compliant diaphragm or turbine gas meter — delivering wireless hourly reads, prepaid balance-triggered valve shut-off, and gas leak alerts to the CGD control room. Ideal for PNGRB authorised CGD entities and Smart City Gas projects.

HourlyRead Interval
PrepaidBilling Ready
<5 sHazard Alert
IS 16687Compliant
  • Automated Gas Meter Reading — Hourly pulse-count push to MDMS — no manual read rounds needed
  • Prepaid Cut-Off Valve — Balance-triggered remote valve shut-off and OTA reconnect on recharge
  • Gas Hazard Detection — Integrated gas sensor triggers immediate valve close and SMS/push alert within 5 s
  • Tamper & Tilt Alarm — Physical disturbance, cover-open, and reverse-flow alerts with tamper log
  • Pressure Monitoring — Upstream pressure logging for network balancing and leak zone identification
