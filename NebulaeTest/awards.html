How We Certify

Our Certification Process

Every Nebulae product goes through a structured pre-compliance → lab → submission → approval pipeline managed entirely in-house by our regulatory team. No external certification agencies needed — we own the process end-to-end.

Managing Your Product's Certification?

Nebulae's certification team manages WPC, BIS, CE, and FCC for client products as part of our IoT Product Development service.

Learn About Our Service →
1

Design-for-Compliance Review

Before PCB layout, our regulatory team reviews the schematic and planned operating parameters against target certifications — WPC frequency band, CE emission limits, FCC Part 15 masks. Compliance issues are fixed in design, not at the lab.

2

In-House Pre-Compliance Testing

Near-field EMC scan and conducted emissions check in our lab using calibrated equipment. Radiated power measurements to verify EIRP is within WPC/CE/FCC limits. Issues are resolved before external lab booking.

3

Accredited Lab Testing

Product sent to WPC-empanelled / BIS-recognised / FCC-accredited TCB laboratory. We prepare the Technical Construction File (TCF) and test plan in advance — minimising lab time and re-test risk.

4

Documentation & Submission

Full regulatory package prepared: test reports, Declaration of Conformity (DoC), technical file, application forms. WPC online portal submission, BIS CRS registration, CE DoC signing, FCC TCB submission — handled in-house.

5

Approval & Ongoing Maintenance

Certificates received, product marked accordingly. We maintain a certification calendar for renewal dates, track regulatory updates (new standards, amended directives), and manage change-in-design impact assessments.
