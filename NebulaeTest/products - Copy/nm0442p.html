About NM0442-P

The NM0442-P Wi-Fi Module is a compact, high-performance wireless communication solution designed for embedded applications. Operating in the 2.4 GHz frequency band, it supports IEEE 802.11 b/g/n standards with data rates up to 150 Mbps.

The module integrates a powerful Xtensa LX6 32-bit processor, large on-chip memory resources and versatile interfaces, making it ideal for IoT and connected-device applications.

With OTA (over-the-air) firmware updates, a choice of u.FL connector or PCB MiFA antenna variants, and extended operating temperature support (−40 °C to +105 °C), the NM0442-P offers flexibility, scalability and reliability for next-generation wireless devices.
