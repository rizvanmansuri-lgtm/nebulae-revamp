Smart Residential

Transforming properties across multifamily, build-to-rent, short-stay, and beyond, Tuya Smart Residential enables the growth of holistically smarter living spaces worldwide, empowering managers, residents, and support staff to deliver more efficient and attractive properties by utilizing a wide range of seamlessly integrated IoT hardware and software tools.

Key Features

Centralized Property Management

Achieve online batch management of properties, units, personnel, and devices; reduce risk of damage with various smart sensors.

Smart Access Control

Enable managers and tenants to better control access to units and common areas; realize functions such as temporary access code issuance and facial recognition.

Smart Home Experience

Redefine industry standards by allowing tenants to move into 'ready made' smart homes; tenants can use the app to explore, create, and personalize their smart living experience.

Smart Energy Savings

Enable greener properties through automated energy saving capabilities for both common areas and units to cut down unnecessary expenditure for tenants and managers alike.
