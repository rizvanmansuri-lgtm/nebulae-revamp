Why Mini DC

Built for India's
charging future.

Every detail engineered for real-world commercial deployment — from monsoon-proof casing to seamless CMS integration.

Dual-Output Power

Simultaneously charge two EVs up to 120 kW total. Intelligent power splitting ensures each vehicle charges at optimum speed without compromise.

7" HD Touchscreen

Vibrant display with multi-language support — English, Hindi, Gujarati. Real-time charging status, usage stats, and integrated payment all in one view.

Multi-Layer Safety

Over-voltage, over-current, short-circuit, and ground-fault protection built in. Emergency stop, surge arrestor, and earth leakage circuit breaker as standard.

Triple Connectivity

4G LTE, Wi-Fi, and Ethernet with automatic failover — the charger stays online no matter what. No dropped sessions, no lost revenue.

Universal Compatibility

CCS2, CHAdeMO, and GB/T connectors cover every EV on Indian roads — cars, SUVs, commercial vehicles, and electric buses with a single unit.

Smart Load Management

Dynamic power balancing across your entire network. Avoid peak-demand penalties by intelligently distributing load without sacrificing charging speed.
