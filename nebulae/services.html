How We Work

Our Engineering Process

A proven 6-step process that takes your idea from concept to certified, production-ready IoT product.

01
Discovery & Scoping
Requirements workshop, technical feasibility, protocol selection, and project timeline definition. Week 1.
02
Architecture Design
Hardware block diagram, firmware architecture, cloud topology, and security model. Weeks 1–2.
03
Prototype Build
PCB design, fabrication, component sourcing, assembly, and firmware bring-up. Weeks 2–6.
04
Testing & Validation
Functional, RF, environmental, and interoperability testing. Bug fixes and performance optimisation. Weeks 6–10.
05
Certification
WPC type approval, BIS, CE, FCC managed in-house. Pre-compliance testing to avoid lab failures. Weeks 8–14.
06
Production & Support
DFM review, production handoff, pilot run, and ongoing engineering support post-launch.
