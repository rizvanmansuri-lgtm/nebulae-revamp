Build and Connect Tracking Devices

Nebulae gateways combine 4G LTE with GPS for mobile and remote installations, and its LoRa and 434 MHz modules provide low-power links for tags and sensors.

The i.MX6ULL system on module and the NebuLink RTUs help OEMs create vehicle and asset trackers with their own software.

Challenges We Help Solve

Vehicles move across areas without Wi-Fi

How Nebulae helpsThe NLG8600 combines 4G LTE with optional GPS for mobile and remote installations.

How Nebulae helpsLow-power LoRaWAN nodes, 434 MHz modules and LoRa point-to-point links keep battery tags running for long periods.

How Nebulae helpsA LoRaWAN gateway collects tag and sensor data across the site and passes it on over 4G.

FAQ

Common Questions

More answers are in our full FAQ, or ask our team.

Do you provide a fleet software platform?

Nebulae supplies the tracking hardware and can design the firmware and application with you. Tell us whether you have a platform or need one built.

Does the gateway have GPS?

The NLG8600 offers optional GPS together with 4G LTE.

What are the options for battery-powered trackers?

The NLN500a and NLN500b LoRaWAN modules and the OTAG-433 434 MHz module are low-power radio options.

Is any specific vehicle-tracking standard supported?

Standards differ by country and device class. Please contact us with the standard you need, and we will confirm what is achievable.
