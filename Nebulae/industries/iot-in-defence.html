Connectivity Where Infrastructure Is Limited

LoRa links reach several kilometres line of sight at low power, IP65 / IP67 gateways can be installed outdoors, and 4G LTE or point-to-point links carry data back to a command point.

Nebulae also designs custom hardware and firmware on its i.MX6ULL system on module for projects with specific requirements.

FAQ

Common Questions

More answers are in our full FAQ, or ask our team.

Do you claim defence certifications?

No. The pages describe general-purpose industrial products. If your programme needs specific approvals, please contact us to discuss what is possible.

What kind of range can I expect?

The NLG7644 gateway is specified for 15 km line of sight and the NLN500a for up to 10 km line of sight. Real range depends on terrain and installation.

Can the hardware be customised?

Yes. Nebulae offers custom embedded hardware and firmware design.

Can data be encrypted?

The NLG8600 supports SSL/TLS, and LoRaWAN includes its own encryption. Additional security depends on your design, so please discuss your requirements.
