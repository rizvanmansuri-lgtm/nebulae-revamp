RF Layout Rules

RF-Optimised PCB Layouts

RF PCB layout is where most IoT designs fail. We follow strict RF placement and routing rules developed from 100+ wireless product designs.

  • Antenna Placement — Antenna near board edge, keepout enforced — no copper, vias, or fill underneath
  • Matching Network Layout — Short traces, minimum bends, component placement mirrors schematic topology
  • Ground Plane Integrity — Solid ground under RF path; stitching vias around RF section perimeter
  • Crystal / TCXO Layout — Short traces, guard ring, no copper pour over crystal body
