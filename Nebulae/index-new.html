LoRa / LoRaWAN

Long Range · LPWAN

Ultra-long range (up to 15km), ultra-low power wireless communication ideal for smart metering, asset tracking, and large-scale IoT deployments.

Wi-SUN

Smart Utility Network

IEEE 802.15.4g-based mesh networking standard purpose-built for smart grids, electric meters, and large-scale smart city utility deployments.

Zigbee

Mesh Networking · Home IoT

IEEE 802.15.4 mesh protocol offering reliable, self-healing networks for smart home, building automation, and industrial sensor networks.

Industry Solutions

Nebulae IoT Solutions Across Every Sector

End-to-end wireless connectivity solutions built on LoRa, Wi-SUN, and Zigbee — deployed across utilities, cities, industries, and homes.

Advanced Metering Infrastructure (AMI)

Nebulae's AMI solution delivers two-way communication between smart meters and the head-end system using LoRaWAN and Wi-SUN FAN protocols. Fully compliant with India's RDSS scheme and IS 16444 standard, it enables 15-minute interval data, remote disconnect/reconnect, tamper alerts, and demand-response — for electricity, water, and gas utilities at scale.

Key Capabilities

Two-way AMR / AMI

Automatic meter reading with 15-min interval data, time-of-use tariff support and real-time load profiling over LoRa or Wi-SUN.

Tamper & Fraud Detection

Magnetic field, cover-open, reverse energy, and neutral disturbance detection with instant cloud alerts — reducing T&D losses by 25%.

Remote Disconnect / Reconnect

OTA-triggered load switch for instant disconnection on non-payment and reconnection upon settlement — no field visit needed.

MDMS & HES Integration

Pre-integrated with major Meter Data Management Systems via DLMS/COSEM, REST API, and MQTT for seamless DISCOM backend connectivity.

Nebulae Products Used
LoRa Module (NB-LRM series) — meter endpoint
Wi-SUN Module (NB-WSM series) — IS 16444 AMI
LoRa Concentrator — DCU / data collector unit
Wi-SUN Gateway — head-end server uplink
Smart City Infrastructure

Nebulae powers next-generation smart city deployments — from adaptive street lighting and parking sensors to environmental monitoring and solid waste management. Our LoRa and Wi-SUN networks connect thousands of city nodes on a single gateway, with centralised SCADA control, real-time dashboards, and open API integration for Smart City Mission and AMRUT 2.0 compliance.

Key Capabilities

Adaptive Street Lighting

Dimming schedules, fault detection, energy metering per pole, and centralised SCADA control over Wi-SUN mesh — achieving 40–50% energy savings.

Environmental Monitoring

Air quality (PM2.5, CO2, NO2), noise, and weather stations across the city providing real-time pollution maps and regulatory reports.

Transformer Monitoring

Current, voltage, temperature, and oil-level sensors on distribution transformers — preventing failures and enabling predictive maintenance for DISCOMs.

Solid Waste & Parking

Ultrasonic fill-level sensors in smart bins trigger optimised collection routes; LoRa parking sensors reduce congestion in city centres.

Nebulae Products Used
Wi-SUN Module — street light mesh node
LoRa Node — environmental sensor endpoint
LoRa Gateway — city-wide network coverage
Wi-SUN Gateway — SCADA head-end bridge
Industrial IoT (IIoT)

Nebulae IIoT connects factory machinery, conveyor systems, environmental sensors, and safety devices into a real-time OT/IT integrated network. Our Wi-SUN mesh modules are purpose-built for noisy industrial RF environments, delivering 99.9% uptime with self-healing mesh topology — enabling predictive maintenance, OEE tracking, and IS 15500 safety compliance.

Key Capabilities

Machine Condition Monitoring

Vibration, temperature, and current sensors detect anomalies on motors and pumps. AI-driven alerts fire before failures, reducing unplanned downtime by 45%.

Environmental & Safety Monitoring

CO, CO2, VOC, particulate, and noise sensors ensure worker safety compliance on factory floors with automatic ventilation trigger alerts.

Asset & Inventory Tracking

LoRa and BLE tags track equipment, WIP inventory, and finished goods in real-time across large warehouse and yard environments.

Energy & OEE Dashboard

Per-machine kWh metering, shift-based OEE reports, and SCADA-ready MQTT feeds for ERP/MES integration with SAP and Oracle.

Nebulae Products Used
Wi-SUN Module — factory floor mesh
LoRa Node — outdoor yard asset tracking
Zigbee Module — inline sensor network
LTE Module — remote plant uplink
Building Management System (BMS)

Nebulae's BMS solution integrates HVAC, lighting, access control, fire safety, and energy management into one unified wireless network using Zigbee mesh and Wi-SUN. Facility managers get a single-pane web dashboard with predictive maintenance alerts, zone-based occupancy automation, and ISO 50001-ready energy reports — all without running new cabling.

Key Capabilities

HVAC & Lighting Automation

Occupancy-based HVAC zone control and adaptive lighting deliver 30–45% energy savings across commercial and institutional buildings.

Smart Access Control

Zigbee-connected door controllers with PIN, card, and biometric access. Visitor pre-registration, entry logs, and remote unlock via cloud dashboard.

Fire & Safety Integration

Wireless smoke, heat, and CO sensors with instant emergency alerts, evacuation triggers, and automated BMS shutdown of HVAC on fire detection.

Energy Analytics & ISO 50001

Floor-wise, zone-wise, and system-wise kWh dashboards with automated monthly energy reports for sustainability audits.

Nebulae Products Used
Zigbee Module — room sensor & actuator mesh
Wi-SUN Module — large floor-area coverage
Zigbee Gateway — floor-level concentrator
Wi-Fi Gateway — cloud management bridge
Precision Agriculture

Nebulae's LoRa-based agriculture solution connects soil sensors, weather stations, and irrigation controllers across farms up to 50 sq km from a single gateway. Farmers access real-time soil moisture, NPK, pH, and microclimate data on a mobile app — enabling automated irrigation, early pest alerts, and yield optimisation that delivers 28% better productivity with 35% less water.

Key Capabilities

Soil & Microclimate Sensing

LoRa nodes monitoring soil moisture, temperature, NPK, pH, and EC with 10+ year battery life and 15km range per gateway.

Automated Irrigation

Threshold-based drip and sprinkler automation triggered by soil sensors. Manual override via mobile app with weather API integration for rain-skip logic.

Livestock & Poultry Tracking

BLE ear tags on cattle with geofence breach alerts, daily activity monitoring, and automated feeding schedule management for dairy and poultry farms.

Cold Chain & Storage

Temperature and humidity loggers for produce cold rooms and refrigerated transport with real-time alerts and immutable cloud compliance records.

Nebulae Products Used
LoRa Node — field soil & climate sensor
LoRa Gateway — farm-wide network
BLE Module — livestock ear tag
LTE Module — remote field uplink

Scene & Voice Automation

Healthcare & Patient Monitoring

Nebulae's BLE and Zigbee modules enable non-invasive, continuous patient monitoring in hospitals, ICUs, and home care settings. From wearable vital-sign trackers and nurse-call systems to real-time asset tracking of medical equipment — our ultra-low power modules deliver months of battery life with zero infrastructure cabling and HIPAA-ready cloud connectivity.

Vital Signs Monitoring

BLE wearables tracking SpO2, heart rate, body temperature, and fall detection — with alerts pushed to nursing station dashboards in under 2 seconds.

Nurse Call & SOS

One-touch BLE nurse-call pendants with room zone identification. Instant alerts to staff mobile apps with patient ID, room, and call type classification.

Medical Asset Tracking

BLE tags on infusion pumps, wheelchairs, and portable ECG devices enable real-time indoor location tracking — eliminating equipment search time.

Cold Chain Compliance
